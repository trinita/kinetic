# Kinetic — design system

## Product context
Kinetic is a small wood workshop (bengkel kayu) in Jatiasih, Bekasi, Indonesia — "the workshop behind Wood Knots" (Instagram @_woodknots). It makes furniture and wood pieces by hand, to order: carved and brightly hand-painted tables and benches (tropical leaves, birds, flowers, butterflies on green/red grounds), with carved diamond-pattern rims.

- No online shop. Every call to action leads to WhatsApp (+62 813-9932-5549). Prices are never shown ("Tanya harga via WhatsApp").
- Indonesian is the main language (English under /en). All copy in drafts should be Indonesian.
- Visitors: homeowners, cafés and interior designers in Greater Jakarta looking for unique, handmade pieces; mostly on phones.
- Jobs to be done: see the work, trust the craft, understand how ordering works, start a WhatsApp chat.
- Key pages: Beranda (home), Produk (catalogue + detail), Layanan (services: Pesanan Khusus, Ukir & Lukis Tangan), Tentang (about + visit), Kontak.
- Hours: Minggu–Jumat 09.00–17.00 WIB, Sabtu tutup. Address: Jl. Wibawa Mukti II No.39, Jatiluhur, Jatiasih, Kota Bekasi.

## Brand
- Logo: a joinery "K" (two wood pieces meeting like a joint) + lowercase "kinetic" wordmark in Fraunces with "BENGKEL KAYU" spaced small caps underneath. Light and dark (on walnut) versions. Always use the supplied logo asset; never redraw it.
- Personality: warm, honest, crafted, quietly confident; colourful work presented by a calm frame.

## Colour
Base palette (wood tones):
- walnut #3e2b20 (primary dark, text-strong, dark bands, footer)
- jati #b07a45 (accent: eyebrows, links, numbers)
- oak #d9b98c (light accent on dark backgrounds, CTA button on walnut)
- krem #f6f0e6 (page background)
- surface #fffaf2 (cards, alternate sections)
- fg #2a1f18, muted #6b5a4c, border #e2d6c4
- lumut #5e6b4a (moss green, secondary accent — currently barely used)
- arang #1e1a17 (dark mode background)

Dark mode: bg #1e1a17, surface #2a231e, fg #f1e8da, muted #bfae98, border #3d3229, accent #d9b98c.

The product photos themselves carry saturated colour (leaf green, coral, pink, yellow, turquoise). Any added accent colour must be taken from those paintings and used sparingly.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces (variable serif), semibold 600; h1 48–64px, leading ~1.05; h2 30–40px.
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans, 16–18px, relaxed leading; muted colour for secondary text.
- Eyebrow: 12px, bold, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.3em, jati colour.
- No other fonts.

## Shape, spacing, depth
- Content width max 1152px, 16px gutters on mobile, 24px on larger screens.
- Generous vertical rhythm: sections 64–96px apart.
- Radius: cards 24px, large images/bands 32px, buttons fully rounded pills.
- Shadows minimal; depth comes from photos and colour blocks, not drop shadows.
- Buttons: primary = walnut fill, krem text, pill, 48px tall, semibold; secondary = surface fill + 1px border; on walnut bands = oak fill, walnut text. WhatsApp buttons carry the WhatsApp glyph.

## Imagery
- Real workshop photos only (no stock, no AI-generated furniture). Available now: painted bench (front + angled close-up), round carved/painted side table (top view, side view, full view in front of a carved wood panel).
- Photos are portrait 3:4. Crop generously; let photos run large.

## Motion
- Subtle: fade/slide-up on scroll (200–400ms, ease-out), slow zoom on hero image, gentle hover lift on cards. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Persistent UI
- Sticky header: logo, nav (Produk, Layanan, Tentang, Kontak), search, ID/EN switch, "Tanya via WhatsApp" pill.
- Floating "Tanya kami" chat pill bottom-right (AI assistant).
- Walnut footer with logo, links, contact, hours, Instagram.
